Super-Soft Burger Buns

Soft enriched buns with a microwave scald

Makes 4 large bunsAbout 5½ h, 40 min of it active

Ingredients

Scald

50 gBread flour16.7%
50 gWater16.7%
150 gMilk50%

Dough

10 gWaterto dissolve the yeast3.3%
4 gInstant yeastor 4.8 g active dry / 12 g fresh1.3%
20 gEgg yolk1 large egg (20–25 g)6.7%
20 gSugar6.7%
6 gSalt2%
250 gBread flour83.3%
20 gButter, coldsmall cubes6.7%

Finish

1Egg white
Sesame seedsto sprinkle

Totals

300 gTotal flour100%
210 gTotal liquid70%
580 gDough weight

Method

  1. Scald. Whisk flour, water and milk in a microwave-safe mixing bowl. Heat in 20 s bursts, whisking in between, until it thickens at 65 °C (150 °F). This takes about 90 s, or a little over 3 min for a double batch.
  2. Cool. Let cool to about 27 °C (80 °F), roughly 45 min. An ice-water bath speeds this up. The scald can be made a day ahead and refrigerated; warm it back up to 27 °C before using.
  3. Mix. Dissolve the yeast in the water. Add yolk, sugar and salt to the scald and stir until dissolved. Add the flour, the yeast and the cold butter cut into small cubes, and mix until no dry flour remains. Cover and rest for 15 min.
  4. Knead. Tip onto the counter and knead for about 9 min using stretch-and-folds. The dough is sticky at first, and the butter works itself in. Desired dough temperature is 25–26 °C (77–79 °F).
  5. Bulk ferment. Return to the bowl, cover and ferment for 2 to 2½ h.
  6. Shape. Divide into 4 pieces of about 145 g, pre-shape and rest for 20 min. Shape into tight rounds and place on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
  7. Proof. Proof covered for about 2 h. Preheat the oven to 160 °C (320 °F) fan during the last hour.
  8. Bake. Brush with egg white, sprinkle with sesame seeds and press them on lightly. Bake for about 25 min until golden brown, rotating halfway through. Start checking at 18 min; the inside should reach 90 °C (195 °F).
Dough temperature: The scald temperature sets the dough temperature. As a rule of thumb, scald temperature = (3 × 25.5 °C − flour temperature) ÷ 2.
Variations: In Europe, use Type 550 (DE), T65 (FR) or strong (UK) bread flour. For active dry yeast, give it 5–10 min to bloom. The dough also makes a soft sandwich loaf in a 1 lb (8½ × 4½ in) pan; bake until it reaches 93 °C inside.
