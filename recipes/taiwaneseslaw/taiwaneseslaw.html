Taiwanese-Style Quick Pickled Cabbage (Slaw Cut)

Yield: ~900g drained slaw • Active Prep: 15 min • Cure: 30 min to 24 hr

Ingredients

Vegetables & Purging

  • 1000g Taiwanese flat cabbage (or green cabbage), cored & finely shredded
  • 100g Carrots, peeled & julienned or grated
  • 22g Kosher salt (2% vegetable weight)

Pickling Liquid & Aromatics

  • 4g Whole Sichuan peppercorns
  • 135g White sugar
  • 150ml Rice vinegar (unseasoned)
  • 12g Fresh garlic (~4 cloves), finely grated
  • 15g Fresh red chilies, thinly sliced
  • 15ml Toasted sesame oil

Instructions

  1. Toast the Peppercorns: Place the whole Sichuan peppercorns in a dry skillet over medium heat. Toss constantly for 1–2 minutes until highly fragrant, then transfer immediately to a small dish so they do not scorch.
  2. Prep & Purge the Vegetables: Set a large mixing bowl directly on a scale and tare to zero. Finely shred the cabbage into the bowl to reach 1000g. Tare again and grate or julienne the carrots to reach 100g. Tare once more and add 22g kosher salt. Massage thoroughly until the vegetables feel damp, then let stand at room temperature (68–72°F / 20–22°C) for 15–20 minutes.
  3. Dissolve & Infuse the Brine: While the cabbage purges, place a microwave-safe measuring cup on the scale. Weigh out 135g white sugar and 150ml rice vinegar. Microwave on high for ~40 seconds until warm, then stir until the sugar completely dissolves without diluting the brine. Stir in the grated garlic, sliced chilies, toasted sesame oil, and toasted Sichuan peppercorns.
  4. Extract Moisture: After 15–20 minutes, the fine-cut vegetables will have released a pool of liquid. Grab handfuls and squeeze firmly to extract as much liquid as possible, discarding the liquid. Do not rinse.
  5. Combine & Cure: Return the squeezed vegetables to the wiped-out mixing bowl or a glass storage container. Pour the warm aromatic vinegar mixture over top and toss thoroughly until evenly coated.
  6. Chill & Serve: Cover and refrigerate. The slaw is crisp and ready to eat after 30 minutes, while the Sichuan peppercorn and garlic notes peak between 12 and 24 hours.
Technique Note: Dissolving the sugar directly in briefly warmed rice vinegar eliminates added water, keeping the fine slaw cut crisp and preventing a diluted, soggy finish.
